## 🏎️ Overview
Royal Safe Drive provides licensed, RTA-certified designated drivers who drive clients safely home in their own vehicles across Dubai and all 7 UAE Emirates. 

### Key Features
- **24/7 Rapid Dispatch**: Strategic driver stations in Business Bay (Ontario Tower HQ), Downtown Dubai, DIFC, Dubai Marina, and Palm Jumeirah.
- **Interactive Fare Estimator**: 1-tap route selection and instant pre-formatted WhatsApp booking link generation.
- **Multi-Service Luxury Packages**: One-Way trips (AED 80), After-Party Drivers (AED 120), Hourly Chauffeurs (AED 45/hr), Full Day (AED 550), Inter-Emirates travel, Monthly Retainers (AED 6,500/mo), Airport Pick/Drop, and RTA Vehicle Passing.
- **Luxury & Supercar Certified**: Experienced with Rolls-Royce, Bentley, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Porsche, and Tesla EVs.
- **Comprehensive Schema.org SEO**: Embedded LocalBusiness, AutomotiveBusiness, and FAQPage JSON-LD schemas.
